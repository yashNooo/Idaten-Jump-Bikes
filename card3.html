Idaten Bikes

Neptune is Makoto Shido's MTB. It can ride through water with greater speed than a normal MTB going through land. It is the 3rd Idaten bike. No one can compete with Neptune in water as its power increases a lot in water. It characterises Sustainability because of it's excellent stability in water. Makoto and Neptune become one while riding. While doing its special move in water Makoto and Neptune are surrounded by water and sometimes take the form of a dolphin. It is known as the Queen of Water.
