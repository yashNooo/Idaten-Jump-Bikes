Idaten Bikes

Thunder Emperor is Kyoichi's MTB. It is the 2nd Idaten bike. Bolts of lightning surrounds it while it is using its special attack. It was destroyed by Koei in episode 20 but it was repaired by Yuki. It characterises Trial skill because it can move well on nearly all terrains except water. It is the 2nd Idaten bike after Flame Kaiser. Thunder Emperor is a trial course champion. In it's full power, the powerful energies surrounding Kyoichi and Thunder Emperor can take the form of an eagle.
