Idaten Bikes

Hammer Head is Arthur's MTB. It is the 6th Idaten bike. The Hammer Crush attack has the power to break through solid objects and gives Arthur more gripping power. It characterises Raw/Explosive power because of its ability to smash through almost anything, and it goes best at Uphill racing. In its full power, powerful energies surrounding Arthur and Hammer Head can take the form of a hammerhead shark.
