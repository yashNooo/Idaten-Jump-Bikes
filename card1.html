The Flame Kaiser

Flame Kaiser is Sho Yamato's MTB. It was handed down to him by his father. It was the first Idaten Bike built by Takeshi for the purpose of defending the world from the Demon's danger. Although, Sho had this bike before entering the X-Zone, the bike is regarded as a legend even there. It holds the one and only platinum emblem, bearing the words 'Flame Kaiser' in red and orange in color and a fire mark.
