Idaten Bikes

Aero Scissors is Koei's MTB. It is the 5th Idaten bike. It's strongest attack is Aeroblade Tornado (also known a Ninjitsu Hurricane) which is used to blow away the competition. Sometimes, the powerful energies surrounding Koei and Aero Scissors can take form of a fox. It characterises Agility because of its ability to ride like the wind.
