Idaten Bikes

Bloody Fang is Gabu Samejima's MTB. It is the 4th Idaten bike. Its strongest attack is Bloody Impact, which creates a red shockwave powerful enough to destroy concrete buildings around him, literally blasting Gabu's competition away. Sometimes, the powerful energies surrounding Gabu and Bloody Fang can take the form of a wolf. It characterises Attack power because of its destructive ability. It is at its most powerful when Gabu is filled with strong emotions.
